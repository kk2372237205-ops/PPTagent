# Quick Slide Rendering

Quick mode uses this compact rendering contract. It is intentionally shorter than the advanced director system so every page stays below Image2's prompt limit.

- Generate one complete 16:9 PPT page, never an isolated poster or illustration.
- Cover and ending: sparse copy, one emotional dominant visual, generous whitespace. Do not force a framed photo.
- Ordinary body page: give the most concrete subject one dominant visual. When the page contains two independent concrete subjects, use two distinct complete photo-like inserts. A chart or fully annotated mechanism may be its own visual.
- A body photo insert must be a complete opaque hard-edged rectangle with a recognizable full subject. Never use a gradient mask, feathered edge, transparency, blue wash, fade into the page, or body text printed over the picture. Place labels and copy outside the frame.
- Render equipment, materials, sites and processes realistically: natural light, honest texture, real material detail. Use a precise technical diagram only for mechanisms, process or data. Never use cartoons, flat vectors, mascots or clip art.
- Keep title, key text and page footer inside a 6% safe margin. Compress copy instead of shrinking the visual.
- Do not fabricate official documents, seals, logos, institution signage, software screenshots, evidence, or identifiable real people.
